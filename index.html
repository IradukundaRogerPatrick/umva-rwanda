You are not alone
1 in 5 Rwandan youth lives with depression, anxiety, or trauma in silence. Umva creates a safe, stigma-free space where your feelings are heard — without judgment.
🤝
Privacy-safe peer counsellors
Our trained peer counsellors come from neighbouring communities — close enough to understand your context, separate enough to fully protect your privacy.
🦻
Inclusive for everyone
Kinyarwanda audio, English text, and Rwandan Sign Language videos — Umva serves every young Rwandan, including the deaf community.
📵
Works without internet
Umva is offline-capable. No internet? No problem. Your wellness data and all content stay available wherever you are in Rwanda.

Get Professional Help
When your peer counsellor or check-in suggests professional support, Umva connects you directly to mental health services covered by your Mutuelle de Santé card — at no extra cost.
💳
Mutuelle de Santé
Mental health services are covered. Bring your Mutuelle card — you do not pay out of pocket.
How the referral works
1
Complete your wellness check-in
Your check-in identifies whether professional support would benefit you. Your answers are private.
2
Discuss with your peer counsellor
Your peer counsellor reviews your situation and helps you decide if a referral is the right step.
3
Receive your referral note
Umva generates a referral note to present at any Mutuelle-linked mental health facility.
4
Access professional care
Visit the facility with your Mutuelle card. Your peer counsellor can accompany you if you wish.
